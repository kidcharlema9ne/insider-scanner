How to read this

Tier / score: the scanner's rating of how strong the buying pattern is. Higher means more insiders, more senior roles, bigger purchases compared with what they already own, and a stock that trades enough to act on.

Insiders: officers and directors of the company. Only purchases they made with their own money in the open market are counted.

Avg daily $ volume: roughly how many dollars of the stock change hands per day. Bigger means easier to buy and sell.

Holdings after / increase: estimated dollar value of what that person owns after the purchase, and how much the purchase added to their prior holdings in percent.

Score breakdown: the points each factor added, what triggered it, and the research it is based on. The weights are priors from the literature, not fitted to our own results yet.

vs 52-week high: how far the price is below its highest price of the past year.

Research leads from public SEC Form 4 filings, not investment advice. Always check the filing yourself.
